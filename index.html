NZ Documents Assistant Apostille & Authentication Workflow
Built for NZ document facilitators & couriers

Run the DIA apostille pathway without re-deriving it every time.

Enter a request once and get the authentication pathway, an itemised client estimate built from your own rates, and the courier and reference support to carry it through — all in one working tool.

  • One pathway checkHague apostille or embassy legalisation, surfaced from the destination country.
  • Fees split clearlyOur own coordination fees stay separate from estimated government, notary, translator and courier costs.
  • Cleaner estimatesAn itemised, client-ready summary instead of a back-of-envelope figure.
  • Courier reachRegional and embassy-city courier contacts without leaving the workflow.
  • Reference on handShort, practical notes on notarisation and legalisation, not a legal opinion.
How it works

Four steps, in the order the work actually happens

01

Enter the request

Destination country, document types and counts, notarisation and turnaround needs.

02

See the pathway

Hague Apostille Convention member, or authentication plus embassy legalisation — with the steps that implies.

03

Review the estimate

An itemised breakdown built from your own rate card, ready to send or print for a client file.

04

Work the network

Pull in a courier for pickup, delivery or embassy drop-off, and check the reference notes as needed.

Not legal advice. NZ Documents Assistant is a planning and estimating tool for experienced facilitators. Default rates and Convention status are drawn from dia.govt.nz, govt.nz and the HCCH apostille status table as of 10 Sep 2026, but none of it replaces DIA, embassy or consular guidance, professional judgement, or your own obligations under NZ law. Convention status, fees and processing times change — confirm current requirements before relying on any figure shown here.
